<h1>The bench, the rig and the programme</h1>
<p class="icl-lead">Community has everything for one engineer at a bench, free and open source. Pro is for the rig: many targets, unattended, with the record kept. Enterprise is for the programme: the whole site, and the evidence for certification work.</p>

<h2>Questions about the editions</h2>
<div class="icl-faq">
<details><summary>Does a licence change what the Community programs do?</summary><p>No. The Community programs do the same with or without a licence, and nothing in them is locked or hidden. The Pro tools are programs of their own, in a Pro package, that drive the same public commands anyone can use.</p></details>
<details><summary>Can I use the Community edition at work?</summary><p>Yes. The GNU General Public License lets anyone use, change and share the code. If you distribute a program that includes the library, the licence's terms apply to that program too. <a href="../licensing/">Licensing</a> has the detail; take your own advice for your case.</p></details>
<details><summary>What is a seat?</summary><p>The terms of Pro define a seat, and your licence file shows how many you have. An Enterprise site licence has no seat count.</p></details>
<!-- OWNER DECISION: the definition of a seat (pro-features decision B: one computer is recommended), and the trial terms (14 days on request, as in Virtual Serial Studio, is the plan). -->
<details><summary>Can I try Pro first?</summary><p>Yes, ask us for a trial licence. The Community edition has every program and library, so you can try the software itself today.</p></details>
<details><summary>What happens when a licence ends?</summary><p>Nothing you made is lost: a record written with Pro can be read and checked with no licence. An Enterprise licence keeps the tools released during its term working after it ends, because a programme that relies on a kit keeps the tool at that version.</p></details>
<details><summary>Does the qualification kit qualify the tool for my project?</summary><p>Your project qualifies a tool, for its own use of it, and your authority accepts that. The kit is the evidence for the tool's side. <a href="../qualification-kit/">The qualification kit</a> says what is in it and who does what.</p></details>
<details><summary>How do I buy?</summary><p>Tell us the edition, how many seats or which site, and which versions you use, and we send a quote. <a href="../support/#talk-to-us">Talk to us</a>.</p></details>
