Qt {{QT_VERSION}}
------------------

The programs use Qt {{QT_VERSION}}. {{QT_DEPLOYMENT}} What they link directly
is Qt Core, Gui, Qml, Quick and Quick Controls 2; their windows import QtQuick,
QtQuick.Controls, QtQuick.Dialogs, QtQuick.Layouts and QtQuick.Window, together
with the platform, image-format, icon-engine, TLS, network-information and
style plugins those need. Which files that is, is decided by Qt's own
deployment tool, which reads the built binary and scans the QML it imports,
rather than by a list kept here.
