Typeface
--------

The programs embed the Archivo typeface (Regular, Medium, SemiBold and
ExtraBold), Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors
(https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo), unmodified, as resources of each
executable. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1,
reproduced below as it ships with the faces. Archivo has no Hebrew, Chinese or
Japanese letters: the windows show those languages in the system's own fonts,
which are not part of this package.
