**The three programs in eight languages** ([docs/translations.md](docs/translations.md)): the data
loader, the media set generator and the mock target each have a language box (the loader's in
Preferences, the others' in the header) with English, French, Spanish, German, Yiddish, Hebrew,
Simplified Chinese and Japanese. The choice applies to the open window at once, including what the
engine said earlier (the result of the last operation, the log, a failure that is showing), and is
kept. Counts are real plurals ("1 load", "2 loads"; one form for Chinese and Japanese). Every piece
of text has a translation in every language, but **the seven translations are machine-drafted first
versions that no speaker of the languages has reviewed**: please report wording that is wrong.
What is still English in every language: the ARINC 665 library's findings, system error codes, what
the target itself sends, and the mock target's protocol log.
