A download that brings back no file (denied, never answered, cancelled, aborted, or a loader that could not run) no longer leaves an empty `DNLD_DATA_<THW_ID_POS>_<number>` folder behind, a `<THW_ID_POS>` that cannot name a folder is refused before anything is made, and two copies of the loader starting a download together no longer fail on the same number ([the loader](docs/app.md)).
