**The mock target no longer crashes when a port one of its transfers needs is already in use.** It writes some of its datagrams from ports of its own. When one of them could not be opened (another program had it, or a second mock target on the same address) the program crashed the next time it waited for the network, and the window disappeared. The datagram is now lost, as UDP allows, and the mock target goes on; the loader's operation may then time out. See [the mock target](docs/mock-target.md).
