The loader fits smaller screens: the minimum window is 1000 by 620 (it was 1120 by 760, which pushed the action bar off a 1366 by 768 display), the activity dock is a share of the window and starts folded in a short one, pages that need scrolling show a scroll bar, and check tiles wrap their detail. Status no longer relies on colour: each state has its own glyph (check, warning triangle, cross) and surface, a finished load is a neutral check rather than a red chip, warnings have their own ink, activity log lines carry a severity word, and the header, the transfer strip and the status bar always agree. See [the design system](docs/design-system.md).
