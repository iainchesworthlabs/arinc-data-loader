**The programs accept licences made for ARINC Data Loader.** Each program carries the four public keys of ARINC Data Loader: two that sign licences and two that sign lists of withdrawn licences. The Licence window and `adl_licence` take a licence file or a list made with one of them, and the edition in the record of `adl_load` follows. A file made with any other key is refused as made for a newer version. A licence still switches nothing off, and with no licence the programs are the Community edition. See [Your licence and edition](docs/user-guide/licence.md#a-licence-the-program-cannot-check).
