**The loader's log and receipt, and the records of `adl_load`, say which edition the program ran as.** The loader writes one line, `Edition: Community` or `Edition: Pro, licence ADL-2026-000123`, at the start of each transfer and under the last line of its receipt. `adl_load` writes the edition, the state of the licence and the licence number in the `licence` key of its operation record, where the file had `null` set aside for it; the file's `format` stays 1, and the events of `adl_load` do not change. The licence is read when the operation starts and nothing is written back. Nothing is switched off by a licence: a licence that cannot be read is the Community edition, and a licence that has ended gives no number. `adl_licence` and `adl_load` now read `ADL_SETTINGS_DIR` through the same code. See [The licence in a record](docs/file-reference.md#the-licence-in-a-record).
