The loader can be operated from the keyboard alone. Every dialog opens with the focus inside it (on its first field, or on its button) and Enter presses its main button where that is safe; the confirmation to stop a running transfer starts on "Keep working". The targets FIND lists are a keyboard list (Up, Down, Home, End, Enter), the two tabs of the activity dock are one control with arrow keys that a click can no longer clear, and the activity log and target configuration scroll with Up, Down and Page keys (Ctrl+C copies the log). Focus is a 2 px ring on every field, steppers and check boxes are at least 24 px, accessible names carry the text on screen, and a field set by voice control or UI Automation now reaches the program.
