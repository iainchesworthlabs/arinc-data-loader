The three programs now use the same words and the same parts: "folder" everywhere (not "directory"),
"Choose folder..." on every chooser, "hardware ID" (and THW_ID_POS for the loader's address string), Cancel,
Abort and Stop each for one kind of thing, one About page with a Licences button, one inline message banner,
one left-rail width, and example text ("e.g. 10.0.0.2") in placeholders that cannot be mistaken for a value.
The loader's receipt records the number of loads ticked when the operation ran and the time it ended, and a
failed download row now says why it failed. The first-run screen says what the defaults really are. The
words are listed in [the design system](docs/design-system.md#words).
