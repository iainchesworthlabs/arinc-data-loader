**Hebrew and Yiddish mirror the whole window** ([docs/translations.md](docs/translations.md)): the
context column moves to the right, the buttons of the header and a choice between segments swap
sides, check boxes, number boxes and progress bars follow the reading direction (a progress bar of
10 % no longer shows 90 %). The two languages are set in the Noto Sans Hebrew typeface, which now
comes with the programs and is listed under About > Licences; Chinese and Japanese use the system's
own fonts.
