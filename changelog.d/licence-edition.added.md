**The programs show which edition they are, and read a licence file.** An edition chip at the right of the status bar of the loader, the generator and the mock target says Community, Pro or Enterprise, and opens a Licence window that shows whose licence it is, for how long, what it includes and why a line is not available, and imports or removes a licence file (`.adllic`). A notice under the header says when a licence ends soon, is in its grace period or has ended, and About has an Edition line. A Support window copies the licence number, the address of the project's issue list and the details of the copy to the clipboard. Nothing in a program is switched off by a licence, nothing is sent anywhere, and with no licence the programs are the Community edition. See [Your licence and edition](docs/user-guide/licence.md).
